Help with your island

Play with friends

Open a Messages conversation, choose Sky Island Trivia from the iMessage apps and start a game. Friends join from the game bubble. Everyone needs the current version installed on their own iPhone.

Each participant chooses a name and animal. On your turn, roll, choose a destination and type your answer. Another joined player judges it. Collect all six category stars and return to the center for the final question. A solo game is available in the iPhone app.

A bubble will not open

Open the game bubble in its original Messages conversation and update the app on every player's iPhone. The current version can read earlier game links; older installed versions cannot read newly shared links. An older bubble may be read-only after you have seen a newer turn. Reinstalling can remove the local player identity needed for your seat.

Report a problem

Contact Sky Island Trivia support on GitHub. GitHub may ask you to sign in. Include what happened, what you expected and, when useful, your app version, iPhone model and iOS version.

Support issues are public. Do not post names, typed answers, full game links, screenshots of conversations or other private information.

Read the privacy policy. Asset credits and full bundled licenses are available from About & Help in both the app and Messages extension.
