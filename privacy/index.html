Privacy

Effective November 2, 2026. This page describes Sky Island Trivia for iPhone, its Messages extension and this public website.

The game

Sky Island Trivia does not use accounts, ads, analytics, tracking SDKs or a developer game server. The developer does not receive your gameplay, player names, answers, contacts or Messages conversations through the app. The game does not request access to your contacts or send your conversation to the developer.

What stays on your iPhone

The app saves solo progress locally so you can resume. The Messages extension stores a random player ID for each game you join, the newest game version you have seen and a board-position snapshot. These local receipts let the extension recognize your seat and older bubbles. They are not purchase receipts.

Starting a new solo game replaces its saved progress. Deleting the app removes its local saved progress and Messages player identity; offloading the app preserves app data. Your Apple backup settings may retain copies. Restoring a backup can restore saved data. After a fresh reinstall, the game cannot recover your previous group seat without that saved identity.

What your Messages group receives

A group game bubble carries a link containing game identifiers, the chosen player names and random IDs, animals, board positions, collected stars, question identifiers and typed answers. Your fellow participants receive this data in the conversation. A copied or forwarded full link also shares its encoded game state. Encoding is not encryption.

Deleting the app does not remove your Messages, anyone else's Messages or previously shared links. Use Apple's Messages deletion and backup controls to manage your copies. Other participants control their own copies. Apple handles Messages transport and applicable iCloud or backup storage under your settings and Apple's privacy terms; this app does not implement iCloud synchronization.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it sends GitHub an ordinary web request, including information such as your IP address, requested path and browser headers. GitHub may keep hosting or security logs under its own privacy terms. This hosting activity is separate from gameplay in Messages.

New game links keep the encoded game state after the # character, in a URL fragment. Browsers do not include the fragment in the HTTP request to the website. These pages use no JavaScript, analytics, external fonts, external media, cookies or local storage, and do not read or forward the fragment. Following a link from this site uses a no-referrer policy. Your browser may retain the full link in its history; do not share that history or full game URLs publicly.

Support reports

If you choose to submit a GitHub support issue, GitHub and the developer receive what you submit. Issues are public. Do not include player names, answers, game links, conversation screenshots or other private information. GitHub may require an account. You can manage or delete your report through GitHub's controls; public copies may persist.
