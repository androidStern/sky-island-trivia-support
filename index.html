Sky Island Trivia

Explore a floating island with an animal companion. Collect stars across six trivia categories, then return to the center for a final question.

Play solo on your iPhone, or take turns with friends in a Messages conversation. Everyone in a group needs Sky Island Trivia installed and updated.

Received a game link?

There is no game account, advertising, analytics or developer game server. Group play travels in your Messages conversation.
